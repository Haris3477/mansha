Summary

The product meets the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed. This means the basic customs duty on your aluminium window frames exported to India will be 0% under the CEPA instead of the normal (MFN) rate of 10%, saving an estimated 10000.00 AED on this shipment. See the checklist below for the steps to claim the CEPA rate.

Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 76101000, which covers doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium). Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

Rule of origin and result

Final manufacture in the UAE More than minimal operations CTSH: 3 of 3 inputs VA 49.0% ≥ 45%

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), where each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, along with value addition (VA) of at least 45%, which is the share of the product's price not coming from non-originating materials. The final manufacture of the aluminium window frames was completed in the UAE, and the operations performed go beyond the simple minimal operations that never give origin on their own. All 3 non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 761010, satisfying the CTSH requirement. The value addition is 49.0% on an FOB basis, which means the price of the goods loaded on board at the UAE port, and this exceeds the required 45% threshold, so the product meets the rule of origin. Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

  1. 1
    Register your factory through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service (moet.gov.ae) before you apply for the certificate.
    Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism · Details
  2. 2
    Apply through the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service (eservices.moec.gov.ae or the smart app).
    Authority: The exporter · Details
  3. 3
    In the application, give the HS code, description, quantity and value of the product.
    Authority: The issuing authority · Details
  4. 4
    Pay the certificate fee: AED 50 for an invoice value of AED 100,000 (fees run from AED 10 to AED 200 by invoice value).
    Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade

Important notes

  • CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, the Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS) and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
  • Duty estimate is on the shipment value given; India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3]
  • The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
