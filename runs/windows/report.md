# Mansha origin check: aluminium window frames

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your aluminium window frames qualify for the preferential CEPA rate under the India-UAE CEPA. For a shipment valued at 100000 AED, this means an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED in import duties. Please refer to the checklist below to complete the steps required to claim this preferential rate.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 76101000, which covers doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium). Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal import duty rate (MFN rate) is 10%, which is charged when no trade-agreement preference is claimed. The reduced rate under the trade agreement (CEPA rate) is 0%. Claiming this CEPA rate for your shipment valued at 100000 AED results in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and a value addition (VA) of at least 45%, which is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials. The final manufacture check passed, meaning the last manufacturing step took place in the UAE, and the minimal operations check passed, meaning the work done in the UAE goes beyond the simple operations that the agreement says never give origin on their own. Additionally, the CTSH check passed because all 3 non-originating inputs were classified outside subheading 761010, and the VA build-down passed at 49.0% against the threshold of 45% on a FOB basis, which is the price of the goods loaded on board at the UAE port. This is based on a price of 100000 AED, 6 total inputs, and 3 non-originating inputs totaling 51000 AED, making the non-originating share 51%. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires factory registration prior to application, which mandates the factory to be located outside a free zone and hold a MoIAT industrial licence with a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The exporter applies for the certificate through the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service on moet.gov.ae by uploading the commercial invoice and proof of factory registration.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee of AED 50 to the Ministry of Foreign Trade for invoice values up to AED 100000.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the Certificate of Origin prior to, at, or within five (5) working days of the date of exportation.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter ensures direct consignment of the shipment from the UAE to India.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the preferential tariff treatment when filing the bill of entry and maintains CAROTAR 2020 origin details; electronic certificates are reportedly uploaded to e-Sanchit.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must retain all origin and cost records for at least five (5) years starting from the end of the year of certificate issuance.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [7][9][6][10][11][15][16]
- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- In the rule of origin, the '+' symbol is interpreted as 'and', meaning both the CTSH and VA tests must pass. Please note that the agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Free-zone producers are likely not eligible for the Factory Registration required prior to obtaining a certificate of origin. [5][6]
