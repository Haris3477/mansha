## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and value addition (VA) of at least 45%, meaning at least 45% of the product's price must come from UAE-originating materials. ## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires the factory to be registered through its Factory Registration service prior to applying for a certificate of origin. Registration requires being located outside a free zone, holding a MoIAT industrial licence, and obtaining a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade provides the Certificate of Origin for India e-service portal, where the applicant fills out the application, submits required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and purchase invoice if applicable), and awaits approval within approximately one working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee within the range of AED 10 to AED 200 depending on the invoice value.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the Certificate of Origin prior to, at, or within five working days of exportation, valid for 12 months for a single importation.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter must maintain all origin records and supporting cost details for at least five years from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims preferential treatment upon filing the bill of entry, maintains origin information for at least five years under CAROTAR 2020 rules, and reportedly uploads electronic certificates to e-Sanchit.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]

## Important notes

- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- A product that only undergoes minimal operations, such as simple assembly or simple cutting, does not originate, even if it meets its product-specific rule. [10][11]
- UAE law requires a minimum 3-year record retention, but CEPA claims must be supported by records kept for 5 years. [10][11]
