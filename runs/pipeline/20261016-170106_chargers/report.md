# Mansha origin check: battery chargers

**Result: Not eligible: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

The product does not meet the rule of origin, so the normal (MFN) rate applies.

## Product classification

Tariff line 85044030: Battery chargers (Mansha's confidence: high). Confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 20%, effective 20% | 0% | none, because the product does not qualify |

Agreement: India-UAE CEPA. Estimated saving: none, because the product does not qualify. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

Rule of origin: CTH + VA 40%. Result: the product does not meet the rule of origin. Final manufacture did not take place in the UAE (FAIL). Declared operations ['b', 'k', 'l'] are all minimal operations under Art. 3.5.1, which never confer origin (FAIL). CTH: 1 non-originating input in heading 8504, together 88.0% of the price, above the 10% de minimis (FAIL). VA: build-down 12.0% against a threshold of 40%, FOB basis (FAIL). Totals: price_basis FOB, inputs_count 2, non_originating_inputs_count 1, non_originating_share_pct 88. [5]

## Important notes

- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3][4]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][8][9][10][11][12]
- The '+' in the rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [5]
- 45/2025 S.No. 323 gives 15% only for EV manufacture; general rate 20%. [1][2][3][4]
- Anti-dumping duty not checked. [1][2][3][4]
- e-Sanchit upload rests on CBIC Instruction 28/2022, read only via LiveLaw/taxmann. [13][14][15]
