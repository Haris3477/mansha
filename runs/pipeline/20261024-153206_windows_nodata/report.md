## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism (must be outside a free zone, with a MoIAT industrial licence and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added); processing takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter the factory's purchase invoice), pay the fee online, submit the application, and print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Certificate of Origin fee: AED 10 to AED 200 (fee range because shipment value is not given).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The certificate is valid for 12 months from the date of issue, covers only one import, and must be issued before, at, or within 5 working days of export; it must bear the authorised signature and official seal of the competent authority.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] Ship the goods directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control, with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry, holds origin information including which origin criterion is claimed, and keeps supporting documents for at least 5 years from the date of filing; electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to e-Sanchit (unconfirmed).  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- Direct labour cost and direct overhead cost are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti-dumping or safeguard duties still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is a prerequisite for the certificate; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- Mansha interprets the '+' in the rule of origin as 'and', meaning both the change in tariff subheading and the value addition tests must pass. The agreement text does not define the '+' symbol. [4]
- When completing the certificate of origin, use box 9 for remarks on India's form, even though the agreement text mentions box 8. India's form governs entry into India. [10][11]
