## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism registers the factory (outside a free zone) before applying for a certificate; registration takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The issuing authority may require detailed cost sheets and a factory visit; each item on the certificate must qualify separately.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade issues the certificate through the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service; processing takes about one working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 10 to AED 200 depending on the invoice value.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate before, at, or within 5 working days of export; it is valid for 12 months and covers one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] The exporter ships the goods directly from the UAE to India; third-party invoicing is allowed if the exporter indicates it.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and keeps supporting documents for at least 5 years.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]

## Important notes

- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][8][10][11][15][16]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- '+' in the rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate. [5][6]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][7][11]
- Operations listed in the agreement as minimal, such as simple packaging, labelling, simple assembly, or simple testing, alone or combined, never confer origin regardless of the product-specific rule. [7][11]
