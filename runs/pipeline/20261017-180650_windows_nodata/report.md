## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires the factory to be registered outside a free zone, with a MoIAT industrial licence and a letter confirming 40% domestic value added, before applying for a certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product, and each item must qualify separately.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade's e-service requires the applicant to log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload proof of factory registration and the commercial invoice (and for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice), and pay the fee online.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 10 to AED 200 depending on invoice value.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade's e-service requires the applicant to submit the application and print the approved certificate after approval, or correct the return with comments.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of export; it is valid for twelve months from issue and covers only one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] The exporter must ship goods directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. Third-party invoicing is allowed if the exporter indicates it.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
