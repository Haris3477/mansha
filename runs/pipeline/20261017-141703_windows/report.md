# Mansha origin check: aluminium window frames

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your aluminium window frames meet the rule of origin under the India-UAE CEPA, so the preferential CEPA rate, which is the reduced rate under the trade agreement, can be claimed. This eliminates your customs duty in India, resulting in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED. Please consult the checklist below to complete the necessary steps to claim this preference.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 76101000, covering doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium), with high confidence. You should confirm this code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the duty is reduced from the normal (MFN) rate of 10% to a CEPA rate of 0%. Based on your shipment value, this gives an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The product must satisfy a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non-originating input is classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, along with a value addition (VA) of at least 45% on an FOB basis, which is the price of the goods loaded on board at the UAE port. The product qualifies because the last manufacturing step occurred in the UAE, and the work performed goes beyond minimal operations. Additionally, all 3 non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 761010, and the calculated value addition is 49.0% against the 45% threshold. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The factory must first be registered through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service, requiring an industrial licence, location outside a free zone, and a value-addition confirmation letter, taking about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade provides the Certificate of Origin for India e-service where the exporter logs in, fills out the application, uploads the factory registration proof and commercial invoice, and submits for approval.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the electronic application to include the HS code, description, quantity, and value, with each item qualifying separately in its own right, and may request detailed cost sheets or conduct a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for an invoice value up to AED 100,000.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate of origin prior to, at, or within 5 working days of exportation, bearing an authorised signature and seal, valid for 12 months for a single import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter must ensure the goods are consigned directly from the UAE to India without operations outside customs control during transit.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the preferential rate on the bill of entry, maintains origin information under CAROTAR 2020 for at least 5 years, and must respond to customs enquiries within 10 working days, with electronic certificates reportedly accepted via e-Sanchit.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must retain all origin records and supporting cost breakdowns for at least 5 years starting from the end of the year of certificate issuance and consent to any verification visits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- The '+' symbol in the origin rule is interpreted as requiring both CTSH and value addition tests to pass, as the agreement does not formally define the symbol. [4]
- Manufacturing operations and final UAE manufacture criteria rely on the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- Duty savings are estimated based on the declared shipment value; Indian customs calculates duty on assessable CIF value, meaning actual amounts will vary. [2][3]
- CEPA tariff preference reduces Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only; other import levies such as IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and trade defence duties still apply. [2][3]
- Producers located in free zones may not be eligible for the mandatory Factory Registration prerequisite, so you should confirm requirements with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
