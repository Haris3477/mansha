## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin is CTSH plus VA 45%. CTSH means change in tariff subheading, which requires each non-originating input to be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product. VA means value addition, which is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials. The product cannot be checked yet because the bill of materials and declaration are missing, so no checks have been performed. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism: Register the factory through the Factory Registration service, which requires the factory to be located outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The issuing authority: Require the application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product, and each item must qualify separately in its own right.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade: Log in to the ministry e-services portal and choose the Certificate of Origin for India service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade: Fill in the application form and upload the required documents: proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and, for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade: The applicant pays the fee online; the fee ranges from AED 10 to AED 200 depending on the invoice value.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade: Submit the application and receive approval, or a return with comments to correct.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade: Print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority: Issue the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of the date of exportation; it is valid for twelve months from the date of issue and for only one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] The exporter: Ensure the goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The exporter: Keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the year of the date of issue of the certificate.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer: Claim the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and hold origin information, including which origin criterion is claimed, for at least five years from filing.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]

## Important notes

- The agreement does not define '+' in the rule 'CTSH + VA 45%'; Mansha interprets it as 'and' (both tests must pass). [4]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][7][11]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][8][10][11][15][16]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources. [12][13][14]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate of origin for India. [5][6]
- A product that only undergoes the minimal operations listed in Article 3.5.1 does not originate, even if it meets its product-specific rule. [7][11]
- The agreement says box 8 for the certificate, but India's origin rules and certificate form say box 9. India's form governs entry into India, so use box 9. [7][11]
