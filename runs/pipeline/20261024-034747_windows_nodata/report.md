## Important notes

- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [5][6][8][9][11][15][16]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- Mansha interprets '+' in the rule as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+'. [4]
- Direct labour cost and direct overhead cost are not defined in the agreement. [4][5][6]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate. [7][8]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to e-Sanchit, but this is seen only in secondary sources. [12][13][14]
- When completing the certificate of origin, use box 9 for remarks, not box 8 as the agreement states. [5][6]
