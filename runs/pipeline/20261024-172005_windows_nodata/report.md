## Product classification

Your product is mapped to tariff code 76101000, covering doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors of aluminium, with high confidence. You should confirm this tariff code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate of basic customs duty is 10%, while the preferential CEPA rate is 0%. The exact duty saving cannot be estimated without the shipment value. The CEPA rate applies only to Basic Customs Duty, so other import charges such as IGST are still payable. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

To qualify, the product must satisfy a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) together with a value addition (VA) of 45%. CTSH requires each non-originating input to be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the finished product, while VA requires at least 45% of the product's price to represent local value. Origin cannot be checked yet because a detailed bill of materials and a manufacturer declaration are required to verify both criteria. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires the factory to be registered through its Factory Registration service prior to applying for a certificate of origin, taking about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade provides the Certificate of Origin for India e-service on the ministry portal for registered local manufacturers and exporters to submit applications with required documents.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include the HS code, description, quantity, and value for each product item separately, and may request detailed cost sheets or conduct factory visits.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee ranging from AED 10 to AED 200 depending on invoice value.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the Certificate of Origin prior to, at, or within 5 working days of exportation, valid for 12 months for a single import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter must ensure goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India and indicate third-party invoicing on the claim if applicable.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter must maintain origin records, including cost breakdowns, for at least 5 years from the end of the year of certificate issuance.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the preferential tariff rate when filing the bill of entry, maintains origin information under CAROTAR 2020 for at least 5 years, and reportedly uploads electronic certificates to e-Sanchit (unconfirmed).  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]

## Important notes

- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Inference: a producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
