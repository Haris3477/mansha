Mansha cannot confirm whether aluminium window frames qualify for preferential tariff treatment under the India-UAE CEPA because required product information is missing. ## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 76101000, which covers doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors made of aluminium, with high confidence. Exporters should confirm this code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 0% | cannot be estimated without the shipment value |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate of 10% is reduced to a CEPA rate of 0%. The normal (MFN) rate is charged when no preference is claimed, while the CEPA rate applies to Basic Customs Duty. The duty saving cannot be estimated without the shipment value. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires CTSH (change in tariff subheading, meaning non-originating inputs must be classified under a different 6-digit subheading than the product) together with VA 45% (value addition, meaning the share of the product's price not from non-originating materials must be at least 45%). The product cannot be checked yet because the required bill of materials and origin declaration confirming the product price basis (FOB) and UAE manufacturing details are missing. Providing these details will enable a complete origin assessment. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires factories to complete Factory Registration on its portal as a prerequisite prior to applying for a certificate of origin, taking about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade provides an e-service for exporters and local manufacturers to submit applications for Certificates of Origin for India with required documents.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate of origin fee ranging from AED 10 to AED 200 based on the shipment invoice value in AED.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the Certificate of Origin prior to, at, or within 5 working days of export, valid for 12 months for a single import shipment.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims preferential duty rates upon filing the bill of entry, maintains origin records for at least 5 years, and reportedly uploads electronic certificates via e-Sanchit (unconfirmed).  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must retain all origin records, including cost breakdowns, for at least 5 years starting from the end of the year of certificate issuance.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- CEPA preferential rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and safeguard or anti-dumping duties still apply. [2][3]
- Mansha interprets '+' in origin rules as requiring both tests (change in tariff sub-heading and value addition) to pass, as the agreement does not explicitly define '+'. [4]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
