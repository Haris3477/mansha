# Mansha origin check: aluminium window frames

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The product meets the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed. This results in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED. See the checklist below for the required steps.

## Product classification

The tariff line is 76101000, described as Doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium). Mansha’s confidence in this classification is high, but the code should be confirmed with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India‑UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 10% and the CEPA rate is 0%. The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) for each non‑originating input and a value addition (VA) of at least 45%. The final manufacture check is passed because the last manufacturing step takes place in the UAE, and the minimal operations check is passed because the work goes beyond the simple operations that never give origin on their own. All three non‑originating inputs satisfy the CTSH requirement, and the VA is 49.0% on a build‑down FOB basis, exceeding the 45% threshold, so the product meets the rule of origin. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires the manufacturing factory to be registered through its Factory Registration service prior to applying for a certificate of origin; registration requires being located outside a free zone, holding a MoIAT industrial licence, and obtaining a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added, taking about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade provides the online service to apply for the Certificate of Origin for India, requiring proof of factory registration and the commercial invoice, with processing taking about one working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for an invoice value up to AED 100000.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to detail the HS code, description, quantity, and value for each exported product, and may apply risk management measures including requesting detailed cost sheets or conducting a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate of origin prior to, at, or within five working days of exportation, bearing an authorised signature and official seal, valid for 12 months for a single import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter must ensure the goods are consigned directly from the UAE to India, with any transit through third countries remaining under customs control without undergoing operations other than unloading, reloading, or storage.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the preferential CEPA rate upon filing the bill of entry, maintains origin documentation under CAROTAR 2020 for at least 5 years, and must respond to customs verification inquiries within 10 working days; electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to e-Sanchit (unconfirmed).  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must maintain all origin records and cost documentation for at least 5 years starting from the end of the year of the certificate's issue date.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- The duty saving is an estimate based on the provided shipment value. India assesses duty on the CIF value at import, so actual figures will vary. The CEPA preference reduces Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS), and trade remedy duties still apply. [2][3]
- Origin qualification relies on the exporter's declaration regarding UAE manufacturing activities and processes exceeding minimal operations. In the rule 'CTSH + VA 45%', the '+' is interpreted as requiring both tests to pass. [4]
- The manufacturing unit must be located outside a free zone to complete factory registration; producers based in free zones may not be eligible to register or obtain the certificate for India. [5][6]
- Acceptance of electronic certificates uploaded to India’s e‑Sanchit system is based on secondary reports of CBIC Instruction 28/2022, not on confirmed official text. [12][13][14]
