## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non‑originating input must be classified in a different 6‑digit subheading than the product, and a value addition (VA) of at least 45%, meaning at least 45% of the product's price must not come from non‑originating materials. ## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism registers the factory before applying for a certificate; registration requires the factory to be located outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added, and takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade issues the certificate through the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service: log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents, pay the fee online, and submit the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 10 to AED 200, depending on the invoice value in AED.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of the date of exportation, and may issue it retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 of India's certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The certificate is valid for twelve months from the date of issue and covers only one import, though it may list several products.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product in the application, and each item must qualify separately; it may ask for detailed cost sheets and conduct a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] The exporter ensures the goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India; if transit occurs, it must be under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar, and the exporter must indicate third-party invoicing if applicable.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information, including which origin criterion is claimed, keeping supporting documents for at least five years from filing and answering customs requests within ten working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- The agreement does not define '+' in the origin rule; Mansha reads '+' as 'and', meaning both the tariff shift and the value-addition tests must pass. [4]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty only; IGST, GST compensation cess, the Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources, not the official text. [12][13][14]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate. [5][6]
- No shipment value was provided, so only the tariff rates, not the actual duty saving, are available. [2][3]
- The 40% value‑added letter required for Factory Registration is a registration condition only; it is not the CEPA product‑specific rule test. [5][6]
- The fee range is AED 10 to AED 200 because no shipment value was provided. [6][7][8][9]
