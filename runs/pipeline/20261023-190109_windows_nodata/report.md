## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin is CTSH plus VA 45%, meaning both a change in tariff subheading and at least 45% value addition are required. CTSH requires each non‑originating input to be classified in a different 6‑digit subheading than the product, and VA is the share of the product’s price that does not come from non‑originating materials. Because the required data is missing, the origin could not be checked and the product cannot yet be confirmed as originating. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism through the Factory Registration service. The factory must be located outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and provide a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Registration takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Choose the Certificate of Origin for India service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Fill in the application form.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Ensure the application includes the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product, and that each item qualifies separately.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] Upload the required documents: proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and, for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Pay the fee online.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The certificate fee ranges from AED 10 to AED 200, depending on the invoice value in AED.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Submit the application. Processing takes about one working day; the service runs 24/7.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Receive approval, or a return with comments to correct. Print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The certificate must be issued before, at, or within five working days of export. It is valid for twelve months from issue and covers one import. It can be issued retrospectively up to twelve months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 of the certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The goods must be shipped directly from the UAE to India. Transit through another country is allowed only under customs control, with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. If a third party issues the invoice, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information, including which origin criterion is claimed. The importer keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing and must answer a customs request within ten working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the date of issue. Refusing a verification visit is grounds for denying preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The issuing authority may apply risk management, including asking for detailed cost sheets and visiting the factory.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule 'CTSH + VA 45%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty only. IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- Value addition may be calculated by build-down or build-up; 'direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources, not the official text. [12][13][14]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate of origin for India. [5][6]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- No usable shipment value was provided, so the duty saving could not be calculated. [2][3]
- Since the IGST base includes the basic customs duty, a lower BCD reduces the IGST amount as well. [14][17]
- You must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years, and refusing a verification visit can lead to loss of preferential benefits. [10][11]
