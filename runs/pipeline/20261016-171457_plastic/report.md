# Mansha origin check: plastic packing articles

**Result: No CEPA preference for this tariff line: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

This tariff line is excluded from India's CEPA concessions, so the normal (MFN) rate applies whatever the product's origin. No saving applies.

## Product classification

The tariff line is 39239090, described as Other (heading 3923: plastic articles for packing). Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 15%, effective 15% | Excluded | none, because the tariff line is excluded from CEPA preference |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 15% and the CEPA rate is excluded for this line. No saving applies because the tariff line is excluded from CEPA preference. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin does not apply because tariff line 39239090 is excluded from India-UAE CEPA preference. The product's origin is not checked, and no preferential rate is available regardless of origin. [1][2][3]

## Important notes

- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [1][2][3]
- Even if the origin rule is met, the normal (MFN) rate applies because this tariff line is excluded from CEPA preference. [1][2][3]
