# Mansha origin check: aluminium window frames

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your aluminium window frames meet the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate of 0% can be claimed. Claiming this rate instead of the normal (MFN) rate of 10% saves an estimated 10000.00 AED on a shipment value of 100000 AED. Please refer to the claim checklist below for the next steps.

## Product classification

Your aluminium window frames are classified under tariff line 76101000, which covers doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium). Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should still confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 10%, effective 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate for tariff line 76101000 is 10%, and the CEPA rate is 0%. Based on a shipment value of 100000 AED, the estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. Note that the CEPA rate applies only to Basic Customs Duty, and other charges such as IGST are still paid. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and a value addition (VA) of at least 45%. CTSH means each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and VA is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials, calculated on an FOB basis. The product meets the rule of origin because the final manufacture took place in the UAE, and the UAE work goes beyond minimal operations. The CTSH check is passed because all 3 non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 761010. The VA check is passed with a build-down of 49.0% against the 45% threshold, on an FOB basis. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism registers the factory through its Factory Registration service, which requires the factory to be located outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a letter confirming 40% domestic value added; registration takes about three working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item must qualify separately in its own right, and the authority may ask for detailed cost sheets and conduct a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade provides the Certificate of Origin for India e-service; the applicant logs in to the ministry e-services portal, chooses the Certificate of Origin for India service, fills in the application form, uploads the required documents, and submits the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for this shipment (invoice value up to AED 100,000).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of export, or retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment with 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9; the certificate is valid for 12 months from issue and covers only one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] The exporter ensures the goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India; if in transit, under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, or storage; if third-party invoicing is used, the exporter indicates it.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry, holds origin information under CAROTAR 2020 including which origin criterion is claimed, and keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing; the importer must answer a customs request within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the year of the certificate's issue; any failure to consent to a verification visit is grounds for denying preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]

## Important notes

- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so the duty saving estimate based on the shipment value given may differ from the actual amount. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- Final manufacture in the UAE and the operations beyond minimal requirements are based on the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically; the India-specific certificate page is still on the Ministry of Economy and Tourism website. [6][7][8][10][11][15][16]
- The 0% CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty only; other charges such as IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti-dumping, countervailing or safeguard duties still apply. [14][17]
- If your factory is located in a free zone, you may not be eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate. Confirm your eligibility with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]

## What's still needed

- Please confirm the HS code for aluminium window frames to ensure correct classification.
- Is your factory located outside a free zone? If not, you may not be eligible for Factory Registration.
