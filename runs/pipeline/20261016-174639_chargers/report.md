# Mansha origin check: battery chargers

**Result: Not eligible: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

The product does not qualify for the CEPA rate under the India-UAE CEPA because it does not meet the rule of origin. Consequently, the normal (MFN) rate of 20% applies, and no saving is available. The main reason is that final manufacture did not take place in the UAE and the value addition build-down of 12% is below the required 40% threshold.

## Product classification

The product is classified under the tariff line for battery chargers, and Mansha's confidence in this classification is high. You should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 20% | 0% | none, because the product does not qualify |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate for battery chargers is 20%, while the CEPA rate is 0%. Since the product does not qualify for the CEPA rate, no saving applies for this shipment. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff heading (CTH), meaning non-originating inputs must be in a different 4-digit heading, and a value addition (VA) of at least 40%, which is the share of the price not from non-originating materials. The product fails this rule because final manufacture did not take place in the UAE, and the work done (repacking and relabeling) constitutes minimal operations that never confer origin. Additionally, the value addition build-down is only 12%, falling short of the 40% threshold, and the non-originating input share of 88% exceeds the 10% de minimis tolerance. [5]

## Important notes

- The origin result relies on the exporter's own declaration of operations and final manufacture. [5]
- The '+' in the rule is interpreted as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+'. [5]
- The duty estimate is on Basic Customs Duty only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4]
- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value, so actual duty amounts will differ from the estimate. [2][3][4]
- Repacking and relabeling are considered minimal operations that never confer origin under the rules, regardless of the product-specific rule. [6][7]
- The general normal (MFN) rate of 20% applies to battery chargers, as a lower 15% rate is reserved for electric-vehicle manufacture. [1][2][3][4]
