## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The factory must first be registered through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service, which requires an industrial licence from MoIAT, a factory located outside a free zone, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade processes preferential certificate of origin applications through the online service, requiring the applicant to log in, select the service, complete the application form, upload the commercial invoice and proof of factory registration, and submit the request.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate of origin fee ranging from AED 10 to AED 200 depending on the commercial invoice value.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate of origin, which is valid for 12 months from the date of issue, covers a single import shipment, and should be issued prior to, at, or within five working days of exportation.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims preferential tariff treatment when filing the bill of entry, must hold origin documentation under CAROTAR 2020, and must maintain records for at least five years from filing.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must maintain all origin records and cost breakdowns for at least five years, calculated from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which requires a factory outside a free zone and must be completed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. You should confirm this requirement with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
