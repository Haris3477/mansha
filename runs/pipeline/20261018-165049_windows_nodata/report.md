## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non-originating input must belong to a different 6-digit subheading than the product, along with a value addition (VA) of at least 45%. The origin result cannot be determined yet because no checks could be run without the necessary manufacturing records. To determine whether the product qualifies, you must provide a bill of materials detailing input descriptions, 6-digit HS codes, origins, and values, plus a declaration confirming final manufacture in the UAE and operations performed. ## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service. The factory must be located outside a free zone, have a MoIAT industrial licence and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Registration takes about 3 working days. Traders can apply only for goods bought from a registered factory.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter the factory's purchase invoice), and submit the application. Processing takes about one working day and the service runs 24/7.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee. The fee depends on the invoice value in AED and ranges from AED 10 to AED 200.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The certificate of origin is valid for 12 months from the date of issue and covers only one import (it may list several products). It should be issued before, at, or within 5 working days of export. A certificate may be issued retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 (Remarks) of India's certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed. The importer keeps supporting documents for at least 5 years from filing and must answer a customs request within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue. Refusing a verification visit is grounds for denying preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule 'CTSH + VA 45%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- A manufacturer located in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required prior to applying for an Indian certificate of origin, so status should be confirmed with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- The 40% value-added letter serves only as a condition for registration and is not a substitute for the product-specific rule of origin required under the agreement. [5][6]
- De minimis provisions apply solely to the change in tariff subheading requirement and cannot be applied toward the value addition percentage. [10][11]
