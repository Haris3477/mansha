# Mansha origin check: aluminium window frames

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The aluminium window frames meet the rule of origin under the India-UAE CEPA, so the CEPA rate of 0% can be claimed. This results in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED for this shipment. Please refer to the checklist below for the steps to claim this preference.

## Product classification

The tariff line is 76101000, which covers aluminium window frames. Mansha has high confidence in this classification, but it should be confirmed with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 10%, but the CEPA rate is 0%. The estimated saving for this shipment is 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and value addition (VA) of at least 45% based on the FOB price. The product passes the final manufacture check, meaning the last manufacturing step took place in the UAE, and the minimal operations check, meaning the work goes beyond simple operations. CTSH is met as all non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 761010, and VA is 49.0% against the 45% threshold. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism through its Factory Registration service: the factory must be outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and provide a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added; processing takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Apply for the Certificate of Origin for India through the Ministry of Foreign Trade e-service: log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload proof of factory registration and the commercial invoice (plus the factory's purchase invoice if a trading exporter), pay the fee online, and submit; processing takes about one working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The certificate must be issued before, at, or within 5 working days of export, is valid for 12 months from issue, and covers one import only; it may be issued retrospectively up to 12 months from shipment marked ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee: AED 50 for an invoice value up to AED 100000 (fee range AED 10 to AED 200).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Ship the goods directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations beyond unloading, reloading and storage. If the invoice is issued by a third party, indicate third-party invoicing.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry, holds origin information including which origin criterion is claimed, keeps supporting documents for at least 5 years from filing, and answers customs requests within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] Keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue; refusing a verification visit is grounds for denying preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule PSR-IN-R118 is read as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Final manufacture in the UAE and operations beyond minimal operations are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ from the estimate shown. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [7][9][6][10][11][15][16]
- If the producer is in a free zone, they may not be eligible for origin because factory registration requires a facility outside a free zone. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
