# Mansha origin check: battery chargers

**Result: Not eligible: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

The product does not meet the rule of origin, so the normal (MFN) rate applies. The final manufacture did not take place in the UAE.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 85044030, Battery chargers, with high confidence. The code should be confirmed with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 20%, effective 20% | 0% | none, because the product does not qualify |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 20% and the CEPA rate, which applies only to Basic Customs Duty, is 0%. Since the product does not qualify, no saving applies. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff heading (CTH) and at least 40% value addition (VA). The product fails the final manufacture check, which asks whether the last manufacturing step took place in the UAE, because it did not. It also fails the minimal operations check, which asks whether the work done in the UAE goes beyond simple operations such as repackaging and labeling, as only those simple operations were performed. Additionally, the CTH test is not met because a non-originating input under heading 8504 makes up 88.0% of the FOB price (the price of the goods loaded on board at the UAE port), exceeding the 10% de minimis (a small share of non-originating inputs that the tariff-shift test is allowed to ignore), and the value addition is only 12.0%, below the required 40%. [5]

## Important notes

- The operations and final manufacture results are based on the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- The '+' in the rule 'CTH + VA 40%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [5]
- A 15% rate under Notification 45/2025-Customs exists for electric-vehicle manufacture, but the general 20% MFN rate applies to this product. [1][2][3][4]
- Mansha has not verified whether India imposes anti-dumping duty on battery chargers; consult Indian customs or a customs broker. [1][2][3][4]
