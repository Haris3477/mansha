## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The factory must be registered through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service (moet.gov.ae). Registration requires the factory to be located outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. It takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product, and each item must qualify separately. The authority may ask for detailed cost sheets and conduct a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the exporter to log in to the ministry e-services portal.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the exporter to choose the Certificate of Origin for India service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the exporter to fill in the application form.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the exporter to upload the required documents: proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and, for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the exporter to pay the fee online.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The certificate of origin fee ranges from AED 10 to AED 200, depending on the invoice value in AED.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the exporter to submit the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the exporter to receive approval, or a return with comments to correct.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the exporter to print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate, which is valid for 12 months from the date of issue and covers only one import. It must be issued before, at, or within 5 working days of export.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] The exporter must ensure the goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India. Transit through another country is allowed only under customs control, with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. If a third party issues the invoice, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed. The importer keeps supporting documents for at least 5 years from filing and must answer a customs request within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years, counting from the end of the calendar year of the issue of the certificate.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the product-specific rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][8][10][11][15][16]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty only. IGST, GST compensation cess, the Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [14][17]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate of origin. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources, not the official text. [12][13][14]
- The agreement says box 8, but India's origin rules and certificate form say box 9; India's form governs entry into India. [7][11]
