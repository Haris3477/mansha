## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and a value addition (VA) of 45%, which is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials. The origin cannot be checked yet because no checks were performed due to missing information, specifically a bill of materials and a declaration. Once these are provided, the CTSH and value addition checks can be carried out. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism registers the factory (prerequisite).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the applicant to log in to the e-services portal and choose the Certificate of Origin for India service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product, and each item must qualify separately.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the applicant to upload proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and, for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 10 to AED 200.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade requires the applicant to submit the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade issues the certificate upon approval.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate before, at, or within 5 working days of export; it is valid for 12 months from issue and covers one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter ensures the goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India, or if transit occurs, under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and holds origin information under CAROTAR 2020.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- No usable shipment value, so only rates are given. [2][3]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- '+' in the rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate. [5][6]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources. [12][13][14]
- The CEPA rate of 0% reduces the Basic Customs Duty and the Agriculture Infrastructure and Development Cess only for lines in Table II. Other charges such as IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS, anti-dumping, and safeguard duties still apply. [14][17]
- While the agreement refers to box 8, India's origin rules and certificate form require box 9; India's form governs entry into the country. [10][11]
