# Mansha origin check: hardwood bedroom furniture

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your hardwood bedroom furniture qualifies for preferential duty under the India-UAE CEPA because it meets the rule of origin. This allows you to claim an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED on your shipment to India. Please review the claim checklist below for details on how to claim this rate. Caution: your factory is in a free zone, so it is likely not eligible for the factory registration needed before applying for the certificate of origin; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism before relying on this result.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 94036000, which covers Other wooden furniture, with high confidence. You should confirm this code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 20% | 12.5% | 7500.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the product qualifies for a CEPA rate of 12.5% instead of the normal (MFN) rate of 20%. This generates an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED for your shipment. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a Change in Tariff Heading (CTH), meaning non-originating materials must belong to a different 4-digit heading, plus a Value Addition (VA) of at least 40% calculated on an FOB price basis. The product meets this rule because final manufacture occurs in the UAE and the work done goes beyond minimal operations. All 2 non-originating inputs meet the CTH requirement outside heading 9403, and the value addition reaches 52.0%. [5]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires factory registration prior to applying for a certificate of origin, which requires the factory to be located outside a free zone and possess a MoIAT industrial licence with confirmation of 40% domestic value added.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [6][7]  
  **Your factory is in a free zone: see the caution in the summary.**
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade provides the Certificate of Origin for India e-service to submit the application along with required documents including commercial invoice and proof of factory registration.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of exportation, valid for twelve months for a single import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [11][12]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for a shipment value up to AED 100,000.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims preferential tariff treatment when filing the bill of entry and must hold CAROTAR 2020 origin documentation, with electronic certificates reportedly uploaded to e-Sanchit.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [13][14][15]
- [ ] The exporter must retain all origin records, including cost breakdowns, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [11][12]

## Important notes

- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [5]
- Operations and final manufacture rely on the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- Factory declared in a free zone: likely not eligible for the ministry's factory registration; confirm with the ministry. [5]
- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3][4]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [7][8][10][11][12][16][17]
