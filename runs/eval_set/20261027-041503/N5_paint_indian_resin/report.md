# Mansha origin check: acrylic paint

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your acrylic paint qualifies for the preferential rate under the India-UAE CEPA. Claiming this preferential rate reduces the duty in India from the normal (MFN) rate of 10% to 0%, giving an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED. Please refer to the checklist below for the steps to claim this preference. Caution: this result counts your inputs from India as originating, which holds only with proof of their originating status.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 32091010 covering Acrylic emulsion (paint), with high confidence. You should confirm this code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 10% while the preferential CEPA rate is 0%. This results in an estimated duty saving of 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH, where non-originating inputs must be in a different 6-digit subheading) along with value addition (VA, the non-foreign share of the price) of at least 40% on an FOB basis (the price loaded on board in the UAE). The product meets this rule because final manufacture took place in the UAE, and the processing goes beyond simple minimal operations. All 2 non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 320910, and the achieved value addition is 65.0%, surpassing the required threshold. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] the Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires factory registration outside a free zone prior to certificate application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] the Ministry of Foreign Trade provides the Certificate of Origin for India e-service for local manufacturers and exporters upon submitting required documents.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] the Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for invoice values up to AED 100,000.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] the competent authority issues the certificate of origin prior to, at, or within five working days of exportation.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] the exporter must ensure direct shipment from the UAE to India and indicate third-party invoicing on the claim if applicable.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] the Indian importer claims the preferential tariff treatment when filing the bill of entry and maintains required origin information.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] the exporter must retain origin records and cost breakdown details for at least 5 years.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
