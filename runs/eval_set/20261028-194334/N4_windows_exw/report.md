# Mansha origin check: aluminium windows

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The aluminium windows meet the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed. This results in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED. See the checklist below for the steps to claim the CEPA rate.

## Product classification

The product is classified under HS code 76101000, described as doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium). Mansha's confidence in this classification is medium, and the code should be confirmed with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 10%, effective 10% | 0% (2026 CEPA rate) | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 10% and the CEPA rate is 0%. The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and at least 45% value addition (VA). CTSH means each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, while VA is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials. The final manufacture check confirms that the last manufacturing step took place in the UAE, and the minimal operations check confirms that the work done goes beyond the simple operations that never give origin on their own. The CTSH check is satisfied because all three non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 761010, and the VA check is satisfied with a build-down of 43.0% against the 40% threshold on an Ex-Works basis. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism registers the factory (requires location outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a letter confirming 40% domestic value added; takes about 3 working days).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The exporter applies for a Certificate of Origin for India through the e-service: logs in to the ministry e-services portal, chooses the Certificate of Origin for India service, fills the application, uploads proof of factory registration and the commercial invoice (and the factory's purchase invoice if a trading exporter), pays the fee online, submits, and prints the approved certificate; processing takes about one working day.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 (band: invoice value up to AED 100000; range AED 10 to AED 200).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of export; it is valid for twelve months from issue and covers one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter ships the goods directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar, and if invoiced by a third party, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry, holds origin information including which origin criterion is claimed, and keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing; electronic certificates are accepted and uploaded to e-Sanchit.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue, and must consent to verification visits; refusal is grounds for denying preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- The '+' symbol in the product-specific rule is read as 'and', meaning both tests must pass. The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, the Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS) and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- By interpretation, de minimis helps only the tariff-shift part of a rule, not the VA percentage. [10][11]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- The Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS) is likely charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text on it was found). Anti-dumping and safeguard duties likely still apply (no CEPA exemption from them was found). [14][17]
- The 8-digit code 76101090 you gave is not a line in India's tariff; Mansha matched it on its first 6 digits (761010). 8-digit codes are national and differ between the UAE and India. [1][2][3]
- The certificate fee is based on the invoice value in AED according to the UAE e-service schedule, and the fee calculation assumes the invoice value equals the shipment value given. [6][7][8][9]
- UAE law sets a shorter 3-year minimum, but the CEPA's 5 years governs CEPA claims. The certificate does not remove the importer's duty of reasonable care, so the exporter may be asked for production and cost details. [10][11][12][13][14]
