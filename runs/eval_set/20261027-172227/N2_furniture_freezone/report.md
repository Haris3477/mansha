# Mansha origin check: wooden bedroom furniture

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your wooden bedroom furniture qualifies for the preferential duty rate under the India-UAE CEPA. Claiming this preference reduces the Basic Customs Duty from the normal rate, giving an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED on your 100000 AED shipment. Please refer to the claim checklist below for the next steps. Caution: your factory is in a free zone, so it is likely not eligible for the factory registration needed before applying for the certificate of origin; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism before relying on this result.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 94036000, which is described as Other wooden furniture. Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 20% | 12.5% | 7500.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate for this product is 20%, while the preferential CEPA rate is 12.5%. Because your product qualifies for the CEPA rate, you can claim an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED on this shipment. Note that the CEPA rate applies to Basic Customs Duty only, and other import charges such as IGST are still payable. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

To qualify, your product must meet the rule of origin, which requires a change in tariff heading (CTH) and at least 40% value addition (VA). The CTH rule means that all non-originating inputs must be classified under a different 4-digit heading than the final product, and the VA rule means that at least 40% of the product's price on an FOB basis must not come from non-originating materials. Your product passes all checks: the final manufacturing step took place in the UAE, and the work done goes beyond the minimal operations required. Specifically, all 2 non-originating inputs are classified outside heading 9403, and the value addition reaches 52.0%, which is above the 40% threshold on an FOB basis. [5]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism through the Factory Registration service (moet.gov.ae). The factory must be located outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and provide a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Registration takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [6][7]  
  **Your factory is in a free zone: see the caution in the summary.**
- [ ] Prepare the application for the Certificate of Origin for India, including the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item must qualify separately.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [8]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal and choose the Certificate of Origin for India service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][9][10][11]
- [ ] Fill in the application form and upload the required documents: proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][9][10][11]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee of AED 50 (band: invoice value up to AED 100000) online.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][9][10][11]
- [ ] Submit the application and receive approval, or a return with comments to correct.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][9][10][11]
- [ ] Print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][9][10][11]
- [ ] The certificate should be issued prior to, at, or within five working days of export; it is valid for twelve months from issue and covers one import. A certificate can be issued retrospectively up to twelve months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 of India's certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [8][12]
- [ ] Ship the goods directly from the UAE to India; if transit occurs, it must be under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. If invoiced by a third party, indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [8][12]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information, including which origin criterion is claimed. The importer keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing and must answer a customs request within ten working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [13][14][15]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [8][12]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [5]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- Factory declared in a free zone: likely not eligible for the ministry's factory registration; confirm with the ministry. [5]
- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3][4]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3][4]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [5][8][12]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [7][8][9][11][12][16][17]
- Inference: a producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [6][7]
- Inference: SWS is charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text found). [15][18]
- Inference: anti-dumping and safeguard duties still apply (no exemption found). [15][18]
- Although the agreement text refers to box 8, India's origin rules and certificate form require box 9 to be completed, and India's form governs entry into the country. [8][12]
- While UAE law sets a 3-year minimum for record retention, the CEPA's 5-year requirement governs your preferential claims under the agreement. [8][12]
