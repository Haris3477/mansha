# Mansha origin check: aluminium window frames

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The product meets the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed. The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. Please refer to the checklist below.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 76101000, Doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium). Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 10%, effective 10% | 0% (2026 CEPA rate, table I, S.No. 8301) | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 10% and the CEPA rate is 0%. The CEPA rate applies to Basic Customs Duty only, so other import charges such as IGST are still payable. The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and a value addition (VA) of at least 45%. The final manufacture took place in the UAE and the work goes beyond the minimal operations that would not alone confer origin. All three non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 761010, satisfying the CTSH requirement. The value added is 49.0% of the FOB price, which exceeds the 45% threshold. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism through its Factory Registration service. The factory must be located outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and provide a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Registration takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Apply for the Certificate of Origin for India through the Ministry of Foreign Trade e-service: log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter the factory's purchase invoice), pay the fee online, and submit. Processing takes about one working day and the service runs 24/7.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Certificate fee: AED 50 (band for invoice value up to AED 100000, based on the shipment value of 100000.00 AED).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The certificate is issued before, at, or within five working days of the date of exportation. It is valid for twelve months from the date of issue and covers only one import (it may list several products).  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] Ship the goods directly from the UAE to India. Transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. If a third party issues the invoice, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and holds origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed. The importer keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing and must answer a customs request within ten working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue. Refusing a verification visit by the Indian authority is grounds for denying preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule CTSH + VA 45% is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual duty amounts will differ from the estimate. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- Inference: SWS is charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text found). [14][17]
- Inference: anti-dumping and safeguard duties still apply (no exemption found). [14][17]
- Electronic certificates are accepted and can be uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system under CBIC Instruction 28/2022. This information comes from secondary sources, not the official text. [12][13][14]
- UAE law requires keeping records for at least 3 years, but for CEPA claims you must keep them for 5 years. [10][11]
