## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and at least 45% value addition (VA). CTSH means each non‑originating input must be classified in a different 6‑digit subheading than the product. VA means the share of the product's price that does not come from non‑originating materials must be at least 45%. ## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism through the Factory Registration service. The factory must be located outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and provide a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Registration takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Apply for the Certificate of Origin for India e-service: log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice), and submit the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee online. The fee ranges from AED 10 to AED 200 depending on the invoice value in AED.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Receive the approved certificate, which is issued before, at, or within 5 working days of the date of exportation. The certificate is valid for 12 months from the date of issue and covers only one import. It may be issued retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 of India's certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] Ship the goods directly from the UAE to India. Transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. If a third party issues the invoice, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed. The importer keeps supporting documents for at least 5 years from filing and must answer a customs request within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue. India may ask the UAE authority to verify a certificate, and the authority must answer an origin verification request within 90 days, extendable by 60 days.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule 'CTSH + VA 45%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because registration requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this comes from CBIC Instruction 28/2022, which Mansha has read only through secondary reports (LiveLaw, taxmann), not the official text. [12][13][14]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only to Basic Customs Duty; other charges such as IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti‑dumping, countervailing or safeguard duties remain payable. Because IGST is calculated on the BCD amount, a lower BCD also reduces the IGST. [14][17]
- The CEPA agreement refers to box 8 for the retrospective issuance mark, but India's origin rules and certificate form use box 9. Since India's form governs entry into India, box 9 should be used. [10][11]
