# Mansha origin check: hardwood bedroom furniture

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your hardwood bedroom furniture qualifies for the reduced duty rate under the India-UAE CEPA because it meets the required rule of origin. This gives an estimated duty saving of 7500.00 AED on your shipment to India. Caution: your factory is in a free zone, so it is likely not eligible for the factory registration needed before applying for the certificate of origin; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism before relying on this result.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 94036000, covering "Other wooden furniture", with high confidence. You should confirm this tariff code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 20% | 12.5% | 7500.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal import duty rate (MFN rate) of 20% is reduced to a preferential CEPA rate of 12.5%. This produces an estimated duty saving of 7500.00 AED for your shipment. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

The product must satisfy a change in tariff heading (CTH), where non-originating inputs are classified in a different 4-digit heading than the product, and achieve a value addition (VA) of at least 40% based on the FOB price (the price loaded on board at the UAE port). Final manufacture took place in the UAE, and the work done goes beyond minimal operations. The CTH requirement is met as all 2 non-originating inputs are classified outside heading 9403, and the value addition reaches 52.0% against the 40% threshold. [5]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The factory must be registered through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service prior to applying for a certificate of origin.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [6][7]  
  **Your factory is in a free zone: see the caution in the summary.**
- [ ] The exporter applies for the certificate through the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service by submitting the commercial invoice and required documents.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for an invoice value of AED 100,000.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate of origin prior to, at, or within five working days of exportation.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [11][12]
- [ ] The exporter must ensure preferential goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [11][12]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims preferential tariff treatment when filing the bill of entry and holds origin information under CAROTAR 2020.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [13][14][15]
- [ ] The exporter must retain origin documentation and cost sheets for at least 5 years from the end of the year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [11][12]

## Important notes

- The '+' in a rule is read as 'and', requiring both CTH and value addition tests to pass. The agreement does not define '+', which is Mansha's interpretation. [5]
- Operations and final manufacture evaluation relies on the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- A factory declared in a free zone is likely not eligible for the ministry's factory registration; confirm eligibility with the ministry. [5]
- Duty estimate is calculated on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. [2][3][4]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti-dumping or safeguard duties still apply. [2][3][4]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [7][8][10][11][12][16][17]
- The India-UAE CEPA reduces the customs duty on this tariff line but does not eliminate it completely. [1][2][3][4]
