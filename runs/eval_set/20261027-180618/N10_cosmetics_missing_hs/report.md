## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and at least 40% value addition (VA). CTSH means each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the finished product. VA means the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials must be at least 40%. ## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The factory is registered through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service (outside a free zone, with MoIAT industrial licence and 40% domestic value added letter; about 3 working days).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The exporter logs into the ministry e-services portal, chooses the Certificate of Origin for India service, fills the application form, uploads required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice), and submits the application.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 (band: invoice value up to AED 100000).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate before, at, or within 5 working days of export; it is valid for 12 months and covers one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include HS code, description, quantity and value, with each item qualifying separately, and may request detailed cost sheets and a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] The exporter ensures direct consignment from the UAE to India (transit allowed under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage) and indicates third-party invoicing if applicable.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including cost breakdown, for at least 5 years from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry, holds origin information under CAROTAR 2020, keeps supporting documents for at least 5 years, answers customs requests within 10 working days, and uploads the electronic certificate to e-Sanchit.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule 'CTSH + VA 40%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- The duty estimate is on the shipment value given; India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only; IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement; the agreement does not say whether the build-down and build-up methods use the same threshold apart from the 5-point Ex-Works adjustment. [4][10][11]
- Interpretation: de minimis helps only the tariff-shift part of a rule, not the VA percentage. [10][11]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- The Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS) is likely charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text found); anti-dumping and safeguard duties likely still apply. [14][17]
- If the production only involves minimal operations listed in Article 3.5, the product does not qualify as originating, even if it meets the product-specific rule. [10][11]
- Cumulation is allowed only between the UAE and India; the final production step in the UAE must still be more than minimal operations. [10][11]
