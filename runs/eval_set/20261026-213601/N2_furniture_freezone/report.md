# Mansha origin check: hardwood bedroom furniture

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The hardwood bedroom furniture meets the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed under the India-UAE CEPA. This results in an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED for this shipment. Please refer to the claim checklist below for the next steps. Caution: your factory is in a free zone, so it is likely not eligible for the factory registration needed before applying for the certificate of origin; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism before relying on this result.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 94036000, Other wooden furniture. Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 20%, effective 20% | 12.5% | 7500.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal import duty rate (MFN rate), which is charged when no preference is claimed, is 20%, while the reduced CEPA rate is 12.5%. The CEPA rate applies to Basic Customs Duty only, so other import charges such as IGST are still paid. Claiming this preference on a shipment value of 100000 AED gives an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff heading (CTH), meaning each non-originating input must be classified in a different 4-digit heading than the product, plus value addition (VA) of at least 40%, which is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials. The price basis is FOB, the price of the goods loaded on board at the UAE port. The product meets this rule because the final manufacture took place in the UAE and the work went beyond minimal operations. All 2 non-originating inputs are classified outside heading 9403, satisfying the CTH check, and the value addition is 52.0%, which is above the 40% threshold. [5]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism through the Factory Registration service. The factory must be located outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and provide a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Registration takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [6][7]  
  **Your factory is in a free zone: see the caution in the summary.**
- [ ] Apply for the Certificate of Origin for India through the e-service: log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter the factory's purchase invoice), pay the fee online, and submit the application. Processing takes about one working day and the service runs 24/7.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][8][9][10]
- [ ] Certificate fee: AED 50 (band: invoice value up to AED 100000). The fee range of the service is AED 10 to AED 200.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][8][9][10]
- [ ] The certificate must be issued before, at, or within 5 working days of the date of exportation. It is valid for 12 months from the date of issue and covers only one import. It may be issued retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 (Remarks) of India's certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [11][12]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed. The importer keeps supporting documents for at least 5 years from filing and must answer a customs request within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [13][14][15]
- [ ] Keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue. Refusing a verification visit by the UAE authority is grounds for denying preferential benefits. The UAE authority answers an origin verification request within 90 days, extendable by 60 days.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [11][12]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule 'CTH + VA 40%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [5]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- Factory declared in a free zone: likely not eligible for the ministry's factory registration; confirm with the ministry. [5]
- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual duty amounts will differ from the estimate based on the shipment value given. [2][3][4]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3][4]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [5][11][12]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [7][8][10][11][12][16][17]
- That electronic certificates are accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system comes from a secondary source (CBIC Instruction 28/2022), not confirmed from the official text. [13][14][15]
- Preference is only given to originating goods shipped directly from the UAE to India. If your shipment transits through another country, it must remain under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, or storage. [11][12]
- The agreement is unclear on whether the build-down and build-up methods use the same value-addition threshold, except for a 5-point Ex-Works adjustment. [5][11][12]
