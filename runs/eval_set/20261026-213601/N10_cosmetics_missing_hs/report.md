## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The factory must be registered outside a free zone through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service before applying for a certificate of origin.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Apply for the certificate using the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service provided by the Ministry of Foreign Trade, uploading factory registration proof and commercial invoices.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Pay the certificate of origin fee of AED 50 charged by the Ministry of Foreign Trade for shipments with an invoice value up to AED 100,000.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate of origin prior to, at, or within five working days of exportation, valid for 12 months for a single import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims preferential tariff treatment upon filing the bill of entry and must maintain supporting origin information; electronic certificates are reportedly accepted via e-Sanchit (unconfirmed).  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must maintain all origin documents and cost breakdowns for at least five years from the end of the year of certificate issuance.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
