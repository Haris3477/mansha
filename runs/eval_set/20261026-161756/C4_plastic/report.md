# Mansha origin check: plastic packing articles

**Result: No CEPA preference for this tariff line: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

This tariff line is excluded from India's CEPA concessions, so the normal (MFN) rate (the standard duty rate when no trade-agreement preference is claimed) applies whatever the product's origin. As a result, the product does not qualify for the CEPA rate (the reduced rate under the trade agreement), and no duty saving is available. Please refer to the claim checklist below for further steps.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 39239090, which is described as Other (heading 3923: plastic articles for packing). Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 15%, effective 15% | Excluded | none, because the tariff line is excluded from CEPA preference |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate (the standard duty rate when no trade-agreement preference is claimed) for this tariff line is 15%, while the CEPA rate (the reduced rate under the trade agreement) is excluded. Since the line is excluded from CEPA preference, no duty saving applies to this shipment. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin does not apply to this product because the tariff line is excluded from CEPA preference. As a result, the origin of the plastic packing articles is not checked, and the normal (MFN) rate applies regardless of where the goods were manufactured. [1][2][3]

## Important notes

- This tariff line is excluded from CEPA preference, so the normal (MFN) rate applies even if the product meets the rule of origin. [1][2][3]
