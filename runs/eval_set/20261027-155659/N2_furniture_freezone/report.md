# Mansha origin check: hardwood bedroom furniture

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The hardwood bedroom furniture meets the rule of origin under the India-UAE CEPA, so the reduced CEPA rate of 12.5% can be claimed instead of the normal 20% rate. For a shipment valued at 100000 AED, this results in an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED. Please follow the steps in the checklist below to claim this preference. Caution: your factory is in a free zone, so it is likely not eligible for the factory registration needed before applying for the certificate of origin; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism before relying on this result.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 94036000 for other wooden furniture, and Mansha's confidence in this classification is high. However, you should confirm this code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 20% (effective 20%) | 12.5% | 7500.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal import duty rate (MFN rate) for this product is 20%, while the reduced CEPA rate is 12.5%. Since the product qualifies, claiming the CEPA rate yields an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED on a shipment value of 100000 AED. Note that the CEPA rate applies to Basic Customs Duty only, and other import charges such as IGST will still be payable. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff heading (CTH), meaning all non-originating inputs must be classified under a different 4-digit heading than the finished furniture, plus at least 40% value addition (VA). Value addition is the share of the product's price, calculated on a FOB basis, that does not come from non-originating materials. The product meets this rule because the final manufacture took place in the UAE, the work done goes beyond minimal operations, all 2 non-originating inputs are classified outside heading 9403, and the value added is 52.0%, which exceeds the 40% threshold. [5]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism requires the factory to be registered through its Factory Registration service before applying for a certificate of origin; registration takes about 3 working days and the factory must be located outside a free zone.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [6][7]  
  **Your factory is in a free zone: see the caution in the summary.**
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product, and each item must qualify separately; it may also ask for detailed cost sheets and conduct a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [8]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade provides the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service; the exporter logs in, chooses the service, fills in the application form, uploads the required documents, and submits the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][9][10][11]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for an invoice value up to AED 100,000 (the fee band for the given shipment value).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][9][10][11]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade approves the application or returns it with comments for correction; the exporter prints the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][9][10][11]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of export; it is valid for twelve months from issue and covers only one import. A certificate may be issued retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 of India's certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [8][12]
- [ ] The exporter ensures the goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, or storage. If a third party issues the invoice, the exporter indicates 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [8][12]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and holds origin information, including which origin criterion is claimed; the importer must answer a customs request within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [13][14][15]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [8][12]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule 'CTH + VA 40%' is interpreted by Mansha as requiring both the tariff shift and the value addition to be met; the agreement does not define '+'. [5]
- The origin determination (final manufacture, minimal operations, CTH, VA) is based on the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- Factory declared in a free zone: likely not eligible for the ministry's factory registration; confirm with the ministry. [5]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti-dumping or safeguard duties still apply. [2][3][4]
- The duty estimate is based on the shipment value given; India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value, so actual amounts will differ. [2][3][4]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3][4]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [5][8][12]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [7][8][9][11][12][16][17]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is from secondary sources, not the official text. [13][14][15]
- If your factory is located in a free zone, you may not be eligible for the required factory registration, which is a prerequisite for obtaining the certificate of origin. You should verify your facility's location status with the relevant authorities. [6][7]
- When completing the certificate of origin, note that the agreement specifies box 8 for remarks, but India's actual form uses box 9. Since India's form governs entry into India, you should use box 9 on your submission. [8][12]
