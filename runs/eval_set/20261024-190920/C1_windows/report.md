# Mansha origin check: aluminium window frames

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The aluminium window frames you plan to export to India qualify for the preferential CEPA duty rate of 0%. By claiming this preference, you can save 10000.00 AED on your shipment compared to the normal (MFN) rate of 10%. Please follow the checklist below to claim this benefit.

## Product classification

Your product, aluminium window frames, is classified under Indian tariff line 76101000 for doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium). Mansha's confidence in this classification is medium, so you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA agreement, the normal (MFN) duty rate for this product is 10%, and the preferential CEPA rate is 0%. Your estimated saving is 10000.00 AED based on the shipment value of 100000 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and a value addition (VA) of 45%. CTSH means each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and VA is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials, calculated on an FOB basis (the price loaded on board at the UAE port). Your product meets the rule of origin because the final manufacture in the UAE passed, the minimal operations check passed, the CTSH check passed with all 3 non-originating inputs classified outside subheading 761010, and the VA check passed with a build-down of 49.0% against the 45% threshold. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service; the factory must be located outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and provide a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Processing takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The certificate application must include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item must qualify separately. The issuing authority may request detailed cost sheets and conduct a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] Apply via the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service: log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the service, fill in the form, upload proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter the factory's purchase invoice, pay the fee online, submit, and print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee: AED 50 for an invoice value up to AED 100000 (fee range AED 10 to AED 200).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The certificate must be issued prior to, at, or within five working days of export, is valid for twelve months from issue, covers one import, and must bear the authorised signature and official seal of the competent authority.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] Ship the goods directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. If a third-party invoice is used, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry, holds origin information under CAROTAR 2020 including which origin criterion is claimed, keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing, and must answer a customs request within ten working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] Keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue; refusing a verification visit is grounds for denying preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]

## Important notes

- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so the actual duty amounts will differ from the estimate shown. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][7][11]
- Interpretation: de minimis helps only the tariff-shift part of a rule, not the VA percentage. [7][11]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][8][10][11][15][16]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources, not the official text. [12][13][14]
- The 0% CEPA rate applies to Basic Customs Duty only; other charges such as IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti-dumping or safeguard duties still apply. However, because the IGST base includes Basic Customs Duty, the lower duty also reduces the IGST amount. [14][17]
- The agreement refers to box 8 for the certificate, but India's origin rules and certificate form use box 9. Since India's form governs entry into the country, you must use box 9 on your certificate. [7][11]
