# Mansha origin check: steel building structures

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The steel building structures meet the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate (the reduced rate under the India‑UAE CEPA) can be claimed. The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. See the checklist below for the required steps.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 73089090, Other structures of iron or steel, with high confidence. Confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4][5]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 15%, effective 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India‑UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 15% (effective 10%) and the CEPA rate is 0%. The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3][4][5]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and at least 40% value addition (VA). CTSH means each non‑originating input must be classified in a different six‑digit subheading than the product, and VA is the share of the product’s price that does not come from non‑originating materials, calculated on an FOB basis. The final manufacturing step took place in the UAE and the work exceeds the minimal operations, which are the simple operations that the agreement says never give origin on their own, so both checks are satisfied. All non‑originating inputs are classified outside subheading 730890, meeting CTSH, and the value addition is 45.0%, above the 40% threshold. [6]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service (requires the factory to be located outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added; takes about 3 working days).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [7][8]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the certificate application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item must qualify separately, and the authority may ask for detailed cost sheets and conduct a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [9]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents (proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice), pay the fee online, submit the application, and receive approval or a return with comments to correct; then print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [8][10][11][12]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee: AED 50 for an invoice value up to AED 100000 (band: AED 10 to AED 200).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [8][10][11][12]
- [ ] The certificate must be issued prior to, at, or within five working days of the date of exportation, and is valid for twelve months from the date of issue and for only one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [9][13]
- [ ] The goods must be shipped directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [9][13]
- [ ] If the invoice is issued by a third party, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [9][13]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry, must hold origin information including which origin criterion is claimed, and keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing; the importer must answer a customs request within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [14][15][16]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue, and must consent to verification visits; refusal is grounds for denying preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [9][13]

## Important notes

- The tariff schedule prints 15%, but an exemption notification lowers the duty actually charged to 10%; the effective rate is used. [2][3][4][5]
- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3][4][5]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4][5]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3][4][5]
- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [6]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [6]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [6][9][13]
- Interpretation: de minimis helps only the tariff-shift part of a rule, not the VA percentage. [9][13]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [8][9][10][12][13][17][18]
- Inference: a producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [7][8]
- Inference: SWS is charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text found). [16][19]
- Inference: anti-dumping and safeguard duties still apply (no exemption found). [16][19]
- Originating materials from India used in production in the UAE may be counted as originating; the final production step in the UAE must still exceed the minimal operations. [9][13]
- The agreement refers to box 8, but India's certificate form uses box 9; India's form determines the entry. [9][13]
