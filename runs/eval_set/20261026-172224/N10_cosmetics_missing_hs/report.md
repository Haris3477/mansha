## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and value addition (VA) of at least 40% of the product's price. ## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism; registration requires the factory to be located outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Ensure the certificate application includes the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item must qualify separately in its own right.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Choose the Certificate of Origin for India service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Fill in the application form.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Upload the required documents: proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee online. The fee is AED 50 (band: invoice value up to AED 100000).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Submit the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Receive approval, or a return with comments to correct.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The certificate must be issued prior to, at, or within five working days of the date of exportation; it is valid for twelve months from the date of issue and covers only one import. A certificate may be issued retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] Ship the goods directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. If the invoice is issued by a third party, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information, including which origin criterion is claimed; the importer keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing and must answer a customs request within ten working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]

## Important notes

- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][7][11]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][8][10][11][15][16]
- Inference: a producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- Inference: SWS is charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text found). Inference: anti-dumping and safeguard duties still apply (no exemption found). [14][17]
- Originating materials from India used in production in the UAE may be counted as originating, and the final production step must go beyond the minimal operations. [7][11]
- Goods must be shipped directly from the UAE to India; transit is allowed only under customs control without further operations. If a third party issues the invoice, the exporter must note 'third-party invoicing'. [7][11]
