# Mansha origin check: aluminium window frames

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The product meets the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed. This results in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED on the shipment. See the checklist below for the required steps.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 76101000, described as Doors, windows and their frames and thresholds for doors (aluminium). Mansha’s confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm it with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 10% and the CEPA rate is 0%, which applies only to Basic Customs Duty; other import charges such as IGST are still payable. The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin is CTSH plus a value addition of at least 45%. CTSH means each non‑originating input must be classified in a different 6‑digit subheading than the product, and VA means the share of the product’s price that does not come from non‑originating materials, calculated on a FOB basis. The final manufacture check is satisfied, confirming the last manufacturing step took place in the UAE, and the minimal operations check is satisfied, indicating the work goes beyond the simple operations that never give origin on their own. The CTSH check is satisfied because all non‑originating inputs are classified outside subheading 761010, and the VA check is met with a build‑down of 49.0% against the 45% threshold. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Ensure the factory is registered through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service, which requires the factory to be outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a letter confirming 40% domestic value added.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Prepare the application for a Certificate of Origin for India, including the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item must qualify separately in its own right.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal and choose the Certificate of Origin for India service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Fill in the application form, upload proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and, if a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee online: AED 50 (invoice value up to AED 100000).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Submit the application and receive approval, or a return with comments to correct.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] Print the approved certificate; it must be issued prior to, at, or within five working days of export.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] The certificate is valid for twelve months from issue, covers only one import, and may list several products.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information, including which origin criterion is claimed.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] If the invoice is issued by a third party, indicate 'third-party invoicing' on the certificate.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] Goods must be shipped directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading or storage.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] A lost certificate can be replaced by a certified true copy.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] An issued certificate can be amended once for a fee of AED 20; the invoice amount and destination cannot be changed.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [15][16]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- Estimate is on the shipment value given; India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][7][11]
- Interpretation: de minimis helps only the tariff-shift part of a rule, not the VA percentage. [7][11]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][8][10][11][17][18]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate; confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources (CBIC Instruction 28/2022 via LiveLaw, taxmann), not the official text. [12][13][14]
- India’s certificate form uses box 9 for remarks, while the agreement specifies box 8; the Indian form governs entry into India. [7][11]
- The issuing authority may apply risk management, which can include requesting detailed cost sheets and visiting the factory. [7]
