# Mansha origin check: aluminium wire rods

**Result: Not eligible: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

The product does not meet the rule of origin, so the normal (MFN) rate applies. The value addition check failed.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 76041010, Aluminium wire rods. Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 7.5%, effective 7.5% | 3.75% (2026 CEPA rate) | none, because the product does not qualify |

The India-UAE CEPA sets a normal (MFN) duty of 7.5% and a CEPA rate of 3.75%. Because the product does not qualify, no saving applies. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and at least 45% value addition (VA). The final manufacture in the UAE passed and the minimal operations check passed, meaning the work goes beyond simple operations. The CTSH check passed because the non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 760410, but the VA check failed because the value addition is only 30%, below the required 45%. Consequently, the product does not meet the rule of origin. [4]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][5][6]
- The agreement does not specify whether the build-down and build-up methods use the same threshold, except for a 5-point Ex-Works adjustment. [4][5][6]
