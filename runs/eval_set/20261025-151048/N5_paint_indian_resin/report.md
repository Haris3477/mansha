# Mansha origin check: acrylic paint (resin)

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The product meets the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed. This results in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED on the shipment of acrylic paint (resin) to India. Please refer to the checklist below to complete the claim process.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 32091010, Acrylic emulsion (paint), with high confidence from Mansha. You should still confirm this code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 10%, effective 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate for this product is 10%, while the CEPA rate is 0%. This results in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED. Please note that the CEPA rate applies only to Basic Customs Duty; other import charges such as IGST are still payable. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and a value addition (VA) of at least 40%. CTSH means that each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the final product, while VA represents the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials, calculated on a FOB basis. The product meets this rule because the final manufacturing step took place in the UAE, and the work done goes beyond minimal operations. Additionally, all 2 non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 320910, and the value addition reached 65.0% on a FOB basis, which is above the required 40% threshold. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Choose the Certificate of Origin for India service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Fill in the application form.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Upload the required documents.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Submit the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Receive approval, or a return with comments to correct.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Certificate fee: AED 50 for this shipment (band: invoice value up to AED 100000).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate before, at, or within 5 working days of export; it is valid for 12 months from issue and covers one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] Keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule 'CTSH + VA 40%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Indian inputs count as originating only with proof of their originating status under bilateral cumulation. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ from the estimate shown. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is confirmed only through secondary sources. [12][13][14]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate. [5][6]
- Interpretation: de minimis helps only the tariff-shift part of a rule, not the VA percentage. [10][11]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- The certificate fee assumes the invoice value equals the shipment value given. [6][7][8][9]
- Preference is given only to originating goods shipped directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. [10][11]
