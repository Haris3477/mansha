## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism registers the factory before applying for a certificate of origin for India. Registration requires the factory to be located outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. It takes about 3 working days. Traders can apply only for goods bought from a registered factory.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product, with each item qualifying separately in its own right. It may apply risk management, including asking for detailed cost sheets and conducting a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [7]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade issues the preferential certificate through the Certificate of Origin for India e-service. The applicant logs in to the ministry e-services portal, chooses the Certificate of Origin for India service, fills in the application form, uploads the required documents, and submits the application. Required documents are proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and, for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice. Processing takes about one working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for the shipment; the fee range of the service is AED 10 to AED 200, based on the invoice value in AED.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of the date of exportation. It is valid for twelve months from the date of issue and for only one import. A certificate may be issued retrospectively up to twelve months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 of India's certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [7][11]
- [ ] The exporter ships the goods directly from the UAE to India. Transit through another country is allowed only under customs control, with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar. An invoice issued by a third party does not by itself cause the claim to be denied, but the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and holds origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed. The importer keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing and must answer a customs request within ten working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years, counting from the end of the calendar year of issue. Any failure to consent to a verification visit is grounds for a denial of preferential benefits.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [7][11]

## Important notes

- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][7][11]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, based on secondary sources, not the official text. [12][13][14]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is required before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- Inference: SWS is charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text found). [14][15]
- Inference: anti-dumping and safeguard duties still apply (no exemption found). [14][15]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][8][10][11][16][17]
- Although the agreement text refers to box 8, India's origin rules and certificate form specify box 9, which governs customs entry into India. [7][11]
- Exporters must retain records for 5 years to support CEPA claims, as the agreement rules take precedence over the shorter 3-year minimum under UAE law. [7][11]
