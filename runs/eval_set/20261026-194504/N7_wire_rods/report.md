# Mansha origin check: aluminium wire rods

**Result: Not eligible: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

The product does not meet the rule of origin, so the normal (MFN) rate, which is the standard import duty when no trade-agreement preference is claimed, applies. The value addition (VA) test, which measures the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials, failed because the build-down is 30.0% against a required 45%.

## Product classification

Tariff line 76041010 covers Aluminium wire rods, and Mansha's confidence in this classification is high. However, you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 7.5% | 3.75% | none, because the product does not qualify |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate is 7.5% and the CEPA rate, which is the reduced rate under the trade agreement and applies only to Basic Customs Duty, is 3.75%. Because the product does not qualify, no saving applies. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), meaning each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and a value addition (VA) of at least 45%, which is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials, calculated on an FOB basis, the price of the goods loaded on board at the UAE port. The final manufacture check, which verifies that the last manufacturing step took place in the UAE, was met, and the minimal operations check, which confirms that the work goes beyond the simple operations that never give origin on their own, was also met. The CTSH test was met because the non-originating input is classified outside subheading 760410, but the VA test was not met because the build-down is 30.0%, below the required 45%. [4]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule 'CTSH + VA 45%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
