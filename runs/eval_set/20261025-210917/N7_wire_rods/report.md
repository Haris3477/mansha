# Mansha origin check: aluminium wire rods

**Result: Not eligible: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

The product is not eligible for the CEPA rate because it does not meet the rule of origin, specifically failing the value addition requirement. Consequently, the normal (MFN) rate applies to your shipment of aluminium wire rods to India. Please review the checklist and details below to understand the status of this export.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 76041010 for aluminium wire rods, and Mansha has high confidence in this classification. However, you should confirm this code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 7.5% | 3.75% | none, because the product does not qualify |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate for aluminium wire rods is 7.5%, while the CEPA rate is 3.75%. Because the product does not meet the rule of origin, no saving applies, and the normal (MFN) rate will be charged on your shipment. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH), which means each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product, and a value addition (VA) of 45%, which is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials on an FOB basis (the price loaded on board at the UAE port). The final manufacture and minimal operations checks passed, meaning the last step occurred in the UAE and the work went beyond simple operations. The CTSH check also passed because all non-originating inputs were classified outside subheading 760410. However, the value addition (VA) check failed, as the build-down value addition was only 30.0% against the required 45% threshold. [4]

## Important notes

- The '+' symbol in a rule is interpreted as requiring both tariff shift and value addition tests to pass. The agreement does not explicitly define '+'. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture rely on the exporter's own declaration. [4]
