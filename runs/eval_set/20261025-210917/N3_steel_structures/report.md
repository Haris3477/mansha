# Mansha origin check: steel building structures

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

The product meets the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed. The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. Follow the checklist below to claim the preference.

## Product classification

Tariff line 73089090 covers other structures of iron or steel, and Mansha's confidence is high. Confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4][5]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 15%, effective 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

The India-UAE CEPA provides a CEPA rate of 0%, while the normal (MFN) rate is 15% (effective 10%). The estimated saving is 10000.00 AED. [1][2][3][4][5]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff subheading (CTSH) and at least 40% value addition (VA). The final manufacture check passed because the last step was in the UAE, and the minimal operations check passed because the work went beyond simple operations. The CTSH check passed as both non-originating inputs are classified outside subheading 730890, and the VA check passed with a build-down of 45.0% against the 40% threshold on an FOB basis. [6]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service (requires location outside a free zone, MoIAT industrial licence, and a letter confirming 40% domestic value added; takes about 3 working days).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [7][8]
- [ ] The issuing authority requires the application to include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item must qualify separately. It may ask for detailed cost sheets and conduct a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [9]
- [ ] Apply through the 'Certificate of Origin for India' e-service: log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the service, fill in the application form, upload required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter, the factory's purchase invoice), and submit the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [8][10][11][12]
- [ ] Pay the certificate fee: AED 50 (band for invoice value up to AED 100000).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [8][10][11][12]
- [ ] Receive approval, or a return with comments to correct; then print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [8][10][11][12]
- [ ] The certificate must be issued prior to, at, or within five working days of export, is valid for twelve months from issue, and covers one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [9][13]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry, holds origin information under CAROTAR 2020, and keeps supporting documents for at least five years from filing.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [14][15][16]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [9][13]

## Important notes

- The tariff schedule prints 15%, but an exemption notification lowers the duty actually charged to 10%; the effective rate is used. [2][3][4][5]
- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ from the estimate. [2][3][4][5]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4][5]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3][4][5]
- Mansha reads '+' in the rule as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+'. [6]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [6]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [6][9][13]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [8][9][10][12][13][17][18]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [7][8]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources (CBIC Instruction 28/2022 via LiveLaw, taxmann), not the official text. [14][15][16]
- Inference: SWS is charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text found). [16][19]
- Inference: anti-dumping and safeguard duties still apply (no exemption found). [16][19]
- India's origin rules and certificate form specify box 9, not box 8, so the certificate must use box 9. [9][13]
- The CEPA's 5‑year period governs CEPA claims, which is longer than the UAE's 3‑year minimum. [9][13]
