## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin is CTSH plus VA 45%. CTSH means change in tariff subheading: each non-originating input must be classified in a different 6-digit subheading than the product. VA means value addition: the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials. The origin result cannot be checked yet because the required data was not provided, so no checks could be run. You need to provide a bill of materials and a declaration showing that final manufacture occurred in the UAE. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory through the Factory Registration service; registration requires the factory to be located outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added, and takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents, pay the fee online, and submit the application.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The application must include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item on the certificate must qualify separately in its own right.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] The certificate fee depends on the invoice value in AED, ranging from AED 10 to AED 200.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Receive approval, or a return with comments to correct; print the approved certificate.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The certificate shall be issued prior to, at or within five working days of the date of exportation, and is valid for twelve months from the date of issue for only one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] Ship the goods directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, storage or similar.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] Keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years, starting from the end of the year of the date of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- The CEPA rate reduces Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is a prerequisite for the certificate of origin. [5][6]
- Interpretation: de minimis helps only the tariff-shift part of a rule, not the VA percentage. [10][11]
- The information that electronic certificates are accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system comes from secondary sources, not the official text of the agreement. [12][13][14]
- Operations listed in the agreement's minimal operations article, alone or combined, never make a product originating, regardless of what the product-specific rule says. [10][11]
