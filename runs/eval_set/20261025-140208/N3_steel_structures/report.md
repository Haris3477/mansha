# Mansha origin check: steel building structures

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your steel building structures qualify for preferential customs duty under the India-UAE CEPA because the product meets the required rule of origin. By claiming the CEPA rate, you can reduce the basic customs duty in India to 0%, resulting in an estimated saving of 10000.00 AED. Please refer to the claim checklist below for the next steps.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 73089090 for 'Other structures of iron or steel', with high confidence. You should confirm this tariff code with Indian customs or a customs broker prior to shipping. [1][2][3][4][5]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 15%, effective 10% | 0% | 10000.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate for this tariff line is an effective rate of 10% (tariff rate 15%), whereas the CEPA rate is 0%. Claiming this trade agreement preference offers an estimated duty saving of 10000.00 AED on your shipment. [1][2][3][4][5]

## Rule of origin and result

To qualify, the product must meet a rule of origin requiring a Change in Tariff Subheading (CTSH, meaning each non-originating material comes from a different 6-digit tariff subheading than the finished good) and a regional Value Addition (VA) of at least 40%. The product satisfies these criteria because final manufacture occurs in the UAE, the work completed goes beyond minimal operations, all 2 non-originating inputs satisfy the CTSH requirement outside subheading 730890, and the value addition reaches 45.0% on an FOB basis (the price loaded on board at the UAE port). [6]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Before applying for a certificate of origin, the factory must be registered through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [7][8]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade receives applications through the Certificate of Origin for India e-service portal, requiring commercial invoices and factory registration proof.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [8][9][10][11]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade charges a certificate fee of AED 50 for an invoice value up to AED 100000.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [8][9][10][11]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate of origin prior to, at, or within 5 working days of export, valid for 12 months.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [12][13]
- [ ] The issuing authority may apply risk management, including asking for detailed cost sheets and visiting the factory.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [12]
- [ ] The exporter must ensure goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India, indicating third-party invoicing on the certificate if applicable.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [12][13]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, keeping records for at least 5 years; electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to e-Sanchit.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [14][15][16]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including cost breakdowns, for at least 5 years starting from the end of the calendar year of issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [12][13]

## Important notes

- The tariff schedule prints 15%, but an exemption notification lowers the duty actually charged to 10%; the effective rate is used. [2][3][4][5]
- Estimate on the shipment value given. India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ. Shown in the shipment's own currency; never converted. [2][3][4][5]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4][5]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3][4][5]
- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [6]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [6]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [8][9][11][12][13][17][18]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, because it requires a factory outside a free zone, and registration is needed before applying for the certificate of origin for India. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [7][8]
