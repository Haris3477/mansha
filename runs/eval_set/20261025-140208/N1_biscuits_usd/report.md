# Mansha origin check: sweet biscuits

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Sweet biscuits meet the rule of origin under the India-UAE CEPA, so the CEPA rate can be claimed. The estimated saving for this shipment is 8100.00 USD. Please follow the checklist below to complete the claim process.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 19053100 for sweet biscuits. Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 30% (tariff 30%, effective 30%) | 0% (2026 CEPA rate) | 8100.00 USD |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate for this product is 30%, and the CEPA rate is 0%. The estimated saving on this shipment is 8100.00 USD. [1][2][3]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin requires a change in tariff heading (CTH), meaning each non-originating input must be classified in a different 4-digit heading than the product, and value addition (VA) of at least 40%, where VA is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials. The product meets the rule because the final manufacture took place in the UAE, the work done goes beyond minimal operations, all non-originating inputs are classified outside heading 1905, and the value addition is 55.0% on an FOB basis. [4]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service before applying for a certificate. The factory must be located outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and have a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Registration takes about 3 working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Apply for the Certificate of Origin for India by logging into the ministry e-services portal, choosing the Certificate of Origin for India service, filling in the application form, and uploading proof of factory registration, the commercial invoice, and (for a trading exporter) the factory's purchase invoice. Submit the application and await approval or a return with comments to correct, then print the approved certificate. Processing takes about one working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Pay the certificate of origin fee, which ranges from AED 10 to AED 200 depending on the invoice value in AED.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] Issue the certificate before, at, or within 5 working days of export. The certificate is valid for 12 months from issue and covers one import. It may be issued retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 of India's certificate form.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] Claim the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed. Keep supporting documents for at least 5 years from filing and answer a customs request within 10 working days.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [12][13][14]
- [ ] Keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years, starting from the end of the calendar year of the date of the certificate's issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]

## Important notes

- India's tariff schedule marks this 30% rate with a footnote that Mansha has not checked; confirm the normal (MFN) rate with Indian customs or a customs broker. [1][2][3]
- The '+' in the rule 'CTH + VA 40%' is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [4]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [4]
- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ from the estimate. The estimate is shown in the shipment's own currency and has not been converted. [2][3]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [6][7][9][10][11][15][16]
- Electronic certificates are reportedly accepted and uploaded to India's e-Sanchit system, but this is seen only in secondary sources. [12][13][14]
- A producer in a free zone is likely not eligible for Factory Registration, which is needed before applying for the certificate. Confirm with the Ministry of Economy and Tourism. [5][6]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [4][10][11]
- Certificate of origin fee bands are in AED and apply to the invoice value in AED. Mansha does not perform currency conversion, so you must provide the invoice value in AED to determine the exact fee. [6][7][8][9]
- For the retrospective mark, use box 9 on the certificate as per India's form, which governs entry into India, rather than box 8 mentioned in the agreement. [10][11]
