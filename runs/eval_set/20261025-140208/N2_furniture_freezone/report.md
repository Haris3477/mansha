# Mansha origin check: hardwood bedroom furniture

**Result: Eligible for the CEPA rate**

## Summary

Your hardwood bedroom furniture meets the rule of origin, so the CEPA rate can be claimed under the India-UAE CEPA. Claiming this preference reduces the normal duty on this shipment, giving you an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED. Please follow the claim checklist below to ensure your documentation is complete for Indian customs.

## Product classification

The product is classified under tariff line 94036000, which covers other wooden furniture. Mansha's confidence in this classification is high, but you should still confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| tariff 20%, effective 20% | 12.5% | 7500.00 AED |

Under the India-UAE CEPA, the normal (MFN) rate for this tariff line is 20%, and the reduced CEPA rate is 12.5%. Because your product meets the rule of origin, you can claim the CEPA rate of 12.5% to get an estimated saving of 7500.00 AED on this shipment. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

The rule of origin for this product requires a change in tariff heading (CTH) and at least 40% value addition (VA). CTH means that every non-originating input must be classified in a different 4-digit heading than the final product, while VA is the share of the product's price that does not come from non-originating materials, calculated on an FOB basis, which is the price of the goods loaded on board at the UAE port. Your product meets the rule of origin because the final manufacturing step took place in the UAE and the work goes beyond minimal operations. The CTH check passed because both non-originating inputs are classified outside heading 9403, and the VA check passed with a build-down of 52.0%, which exceeds the 40% threshold. [5]

## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] The Ministry of Economy and Tourism registers the factory (requires location outside a free zone, a MoIAT industrial licence, and a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added; takes about 3 working days).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [6][7]
- [ ] The Ministry of Foreign Trade issues the Certificate of Origin for India through its e-service: log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload the required documents, pay the fee online, submit the application, and receive approval or a return with comments to correct; processing takes about one working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][8][9][10]
- [ ] Certificate fee: AED 50 (band: invoice value up to AED 100000).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [7][8][9][10]
- [ ] The competent authority issues the certificate prior to, at, or within five working days of the date of exportation; it is valid for twelve months from the date of issue and covers only one import.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [11][12]
- [ ] The Indian importer claims the CEPA rate when filing the bill of entry and must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including which origin criterion is claimed.  
  *Authority: The Indian importer.* [13][14][15]
- [ ] The exporter keeps origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least five years starting from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [11][12]

## Important notes

- The '+' in the product-specific rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass); the agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [5]
- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- Factory declared in a free zone: likely not eligible for the ministry's factory registration; confirm with the ministry. [5]
- India charges duty on the assessable (CIF) value at import, so actual amounts will differ from the estimate shown. [2][3][4]
- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only. IGST, GST compensation cess, SWS and any anti-dumping or safeguard duty still apply. [2][3][4]
- The CEPA rate applies only if the product qualifies under the rules of origin and a valid certificate of origin is presented. [2][3][4]
- 'Direct labour cost' and 'direct overhead cost' are not defined in the agreement. [5][11][12]
- No official page read names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically: its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page is still on moet.gov.ae. [7][8][10][11][12][16][17]
- Inference: SWS is charged on the reduced BCD (no CEPA-specific text found). [15][18]
- Inference: anti-dumping and safeguard duties still apply (no exemption found). [15][18]
- The CEPA lowers the import duty on this product but does not remove it entirely. [1][2][3][4]
- While the agreement text mentions box 8, India's certificate form requires box 9 to be completed for entry into India. [11][12]
