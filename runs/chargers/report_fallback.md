# Mansha origin check: battery chargers

**Result: Not eligible: the normal (MFN) rate applies**

## Summary

The product does not meet the rule of origin, so the normal (MFN) rate applies. The checks that failed are listed under the rule of origin below.

## Product classification

Tariff line 85044030: Battery chargers (Mansha's confidence: high). Confirm the code with Indian customs or a customs broker before shipping. [1][2][3][4]

## Duty comparison

| Normal (MFN) rate | CEPA rate | Estimated saving |
|---|---|---|
| 20% | 0% | none, because the product does not qualify |

Agreement: India-UAE CEPA. Estimated saving: none, because the product does not qualify. [1][2][3][4]

## Rule of origin and result

Rule of origin: CTH + VA 40%. Result: the product does not meet the rule of origin. Final manufacture did not take place in the UAE. The declared operations (repacking and relabeling) are minimal operations that never confer origin. One non-originating input falls in the same heading 8504 as the finished battery chargers and accounts for 88.0% of the FOB price, above the 10% de minimis tolerance. Value addition calculated by build-down is 12.0% against a required threshold of 40% on an FOB basis. [5]

## Important notes

- Operations and final manufacture are the exporter's own declaration. [5]
- '+' in a rule is read as 'and' (both tests must pass). The agreement does not define '+', so this is Mansha's interpretation. [5]
- A product that only undergoes these operations does not originate, even if it meets its product-specific rule. [6][7]
- 45/2025 S.No. 323 gives 15% only for EV manufacture; general rate 20%. [1][2][3][4]
