## Step-by-step checklist to claim the CEPA rate

- [ ] Register the factory through the Ministry of Economy and Tourism's Factory Registration service (moet.gov.ae); the factory must be located outside a free zone, hold a MoIAT industrial licence, and obtain a MoIAT letter confirming 40% domestic value added. Registration takes about 3 working days.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Economy and Tourism.* [5][6]
- [ ] Log in to the ministry e-services portal, choose the Certificate of Origin for India service, fill in the application form, upload required documents (proof of factory registration, commercial invoice, and for a trading exporter the factory's purchase invoice), pay the fee online, and submit the application. Processing takes about one working day.  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]
- [ ] The certificate of origin is valid for 12 months from the date of issue, covers one import (may list several products), and must be issued prior to, at, or within 5 working days of the date of exportation. It bears the authorised signature and official seal of the competent authority.  
  *Authority: The competent authority.* [10][11]
- [ ] The application must include the HS code, description, quantity and value of the exported product; each item must qualify separately. The issuing authority may apply risk management, including requesting detailed cost sheets and conducting a factory visit.  
  *Authority: The issuing authority.* [10]
- [ ] The exporter must ensure the goods are shipped directly from the UAE to India; transit through another country is allowed only under customs control with no operations other than unloading, reloading, or storage. If a third-party invoice is used, the exporter must indicate 'third-party invoicing'.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] The exporter must keep origin records, including the cost breakdown, for at least 5 years from the end of the calendar year of the certificate's issue. India may request verification; the UAE authority answers within 90 days (extendable by 60). Refusing a verification visit is grounds for denying the preference.  
  *Authority: The exporter.* [10][11]
- [ ] Pay the certificate-of-origin fee, which ranges from AED 10 to AED 200 depending on the invoice value in AED (fee bands per Cabinet Resolution 208/2025).  
  *Authority: The Ministry of Foreign Trade.* [6][7][8][9]

## Important notes

- CEPA rates reduce Basic Customs Duty (BCD) only; IGST, GST compensation cess, Social Welfare Surcharge, and any anti-dumping, countervailing, or safeguard duties still apply. [2][3]
- No official page names the Ministry of Foreign Trade as the issuer of the India CEPA certificate specifically; its service page covers preferential certificates in general, and the India-specific page remains on moet.gov.ae. [7][9][6][10][11][12][13]
- The '+' in the product-specific rule is interpreted as 'and', meaning both the tariff shift and the value-addition test must pass. [4]
- De minimis tolerance allows up to 10% of the FOB value or Ex-Works price to be non-originating material; for wholly obtained products the limit is 1%. [10][11]
- A certificate of origin may be issued retrospectively up to 12 months from the date of shipment, marked 'ISSUED RETROSPECTIVELY' in box 9 (Remarks) of India's certificate form. [10][11]
- The Indian importer must hold origin information under CAROTAR 2020, including the origin criterion claimed, keep supporting documents for at least 5 years, and respond to customs requests within 10 working days. [14][15][16]
